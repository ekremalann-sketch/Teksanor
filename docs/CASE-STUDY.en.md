# Teksanor — Executive Product Case Study

## Executive summary

Teksanor is a working product prototype for technical service, maintenance and engineering operations. It connects work requests, assets, field evidence, service records and operational finance in a role-aware workspace.

## Business problem

Small and mid-sized technical organisations often manage work through spreadsheets, messaging, email and paper forms. This fragmentation makes ownership, status, cost and audit history difficult to see.

## Product response

Teksanor creates one traceable operational chain:

**Request → work order → team and asset → field evidence → service record → payment follow-up and audit history**

## Intended users

- Technical service and maintenance leaders
- Field technicians and engineers
- Operations and finance coordinators
- Small and mid-sized engineering organisations

## Evidence available today

| Capability | Current evidence | Honest limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Work orders and field operations | Working prototype flows | Must be accepted in the customer's environment |
| Assets and preventive maintenance | Inventory, schedule and history model | Device and offline testing remain pilot work |
| Role and organisation boundaries | Implemented access model and tests | Full role matrix needs target-environment verification |
| Operational finance | Payment, liability and expense visibility | Not an official accounting or banking product |
| Deployment quality | Automated build, security and secret checks | Independent assessment still required before production |

## Pilot proposition

Start with one operational team and one measurable workflow for 6–12 weeks. Define users, data boundaries, baseline metrics, acceptance tests, support scope and exit criteria before implementation.

## Example success measures

- Time required to locate the current owner and status of a job
- Percentage of planned maintenance completed on time
- Time required to retrieve a service record and evidence
- Number of duplicate manual entries eliminated
- Authorisation tests passed across organisation and role boundaries

## Commercial paths

1. Paid discovery and controlled pilot
2. Customer-specific implementation and integration
3. Support and maintenance agreement
4. Subscription model only after production gates are proven

## Decision statement

Teksanor should be evaluated as a credible pilot candidate and engineering portfolio product—not as a finished global SaaS. Its value is the connected workflow and disciplined path to production, not the number of menu items.
