# Teksanor Demo Guide

## Five-minute executive tour

1. Open the [English product page](https://teksanor.pages.dev/en).
2. Review the product promise: connected field work and accountable decisions.
3. Follow the core chain from work request to service record.
4. Inspect the trust statement and production limitations.
5. Review the [executive case study](CASE-STUDY.en.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## Questions the demo answers

- Can the product connect assets, field work and maintenance history?
- Can operational and financial visibility be separated by role?
- Is the deployment and security process documented?
- Are prototype limitations stated honestly?
- Is there a measurable route to a controlled pilot?

## Safe-use rule

Use only synthetic data. Do not enter customer, employee, identity, financial, credential or confidential business data into the public demonstration.
