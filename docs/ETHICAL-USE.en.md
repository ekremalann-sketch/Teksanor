# Ethical and Responsible Use Policy

Teksanor is designed to organise work orders, maintenance, assets, documents and operational records. It must not be used for covert employee surveillance, discriminatory decisions or high-impact automated decisions without accountable human review.

## Principles

1. Process data only for a defined and legitimate operational purpose.
2. Collect the minimum data required.
3. Tell users what is processed, why and who can access it.
4. Keep a qualified human responsible for material decisions.
5. Limit every user to the records required by role and organisation.
6. Never present missing, inferred or unverified data as confirmed fact.
7. Protect credentials and maintain appropriate audit records.
8. Provide correction, retention and deletion processes.
9. Assess external processors, transfers and storage terms.
10. Keep essential workflows accessible.

## Prohibited uses

Unauthorised surveillance or profiling; discriminatory employment decisions; credential harvesting; fabricated records; bypassing tenant or role boundaries; unlawful data transfer; indefinite retention without purpose; and presenting automated output as a verified human decision.

## AI and automation

When optional AI features are enabled, their source, limitations and human-review requirement must be visible. Personal data or trade secrets must not be sent to an external model without an authorised purpose and appropriate safeguards. Production use requires access limits, cost limits, logging and accountable approval.

Before real-customer use, verify applicable privacy, employment, consumer, cybersecurity and AI rules for every target jurisdiction. This policy is a product-governance baseline, not legal advice.
